Data & Deletion

Local history: open History to inspect records. Swipe a row to delete one scan, or use the menu to export or clear all history. Deletion removes the stored thumbnail and report, not the source photo in your library. It cannot be undone in the app.

Copies: clearing history does not remove report cards saved to Photos, exported files, support attachments or copies shared with others. Remove those separately. Temporary report and export files may remain in the app cache until system cleanup or app removal. An unread shared image remains in the App Group inbox until the app consumes it. Apple-managed device backups must be managed separately.

Cloud control: withdraw Cloud Scan Permission in Settings to block future photo uploads. Offline Mode also blocks cloud scans. Neither action deletes previously cached results, transaction records or an already submitted image from a provider. Cache and counter retention are explained in the Privacy Policy.

No registered login account exists. Credits use an anonymous account ID; the ID can survive app removal through Keychain and may sync to other devices. Removing the app or deleting history does not close this ledger or cancel/refund a purchase. There is currently no in-app server-account deletion button.

To request access, correction or deletion of server data, contact treeui.old@gmail.com with the request first. We may privately request your backup code to locate and verify the account. Do not post it publicly. We will explain how deletion affects remaining credits and what transaction records must be retained. Hive and Apple data are also subject to their own procedures; third-party deletion is not automatically completed by a local history deletion.
