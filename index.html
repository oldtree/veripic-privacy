VeriPic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

VeriPic checks whether a photo looks AI-generated. It was built so that your photos never leave your device. This page explains exactly what happens.

What we collect

Nothing. VeriPic does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, and no advertising SDKs in this version.

What happens to your photos

  • Analysis is on-device. Metadata inspection, texture analysis and the AI model all run locally on your iPhone.
  • Screenshots and history are local. Scan results and small thumbnails are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • No uploads. The Share extension and all scanning work without a network connection.
  • Shared photos stay on this device. An image you share to VeriPic is kept briefly in the app’s private storage on your phone and deleted after it is analyzed.

Permissions

The app uses the photo picker provided by the system; VeriPic itself is never granted full access to your photo library.

Children

The app collects no data and is safe for all ages (rated 4+).

Changes

If a future version adds optional features that process images off-device (for example a cloud deep scan), this policy will be updated and the feature will be clearly labeled, off by default, and explained in the App Store release notes before it ships.

VeriPic is an assistive tool. Detection results are probabilistic and provided for reference only; they are not proof of authenticity.
